When I redid this site I went black and white for basically everything, so the
only color left is the little picture next to each post. Those are all generated
in code -- there aren't any image files, each one gets drawn from the post when
the site builds.

The first version worked out the shape and colors from the post's name. That
seemed fine until I looked at the whole list together and one shape was on 4 of
13 posts, and 7 of 13 were some kind of blue-purple. So I learned that a
scrambled name doesn't actually spread things out, it just kind of clumps.

Now it goes by order instead. The oldest post gets the first shape and color,
the next post gets the next one, and it keeps going around the color wheel from
there, which means two posts next to each other never look alike. The name still
gets used for the small stuff, like where the shapes sit, so two posts with the
same shape still come out different. And new posts just get added on the end, so
publishing something doesn't change any of the old pictures.
