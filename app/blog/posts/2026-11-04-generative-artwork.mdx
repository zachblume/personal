When I redesigned this site I made everything black and white, except for one
thing. Every post gets a little picture next to it, and that's the only color on
the page.

Nobody draws them. Each one is generated from the post itself.

The first version worked the colors out from each post's name. Any one of them
looked fine, but put them all in a list and something was off: four posts had
the same shape, and seven out of thirteen were some shade of blue-purple. Random
isn't the same as spread out.

The fix was to give each post its place in line instead. The first post gets the
first shape and color, the next post gets the next one, and so on around the
color wheel, so neighbors never match. The name still nudges the details, so no
two come out the same. And since new posts go on the end, publishing never
changes the picture of an old one.

Here's the thing that draws them. Type anything, step through, and if you find
one you like, download it as a wallpaper.
